## 2026-08-11 - Optimized CleanYAMLNullValues with Fast-Path and Scan
**Learning:** `CleanYAMLNullValues` originally utilized regex-based replacements and `strings.Split`/`strings.Join` line-by-line scanning, leading to multiple high-overhead heap allocations per operation (~29-36 allocations). Introducing a quick `strings.Contains` fast-path completely bypasses the scanner when no target substring exists. For strings requiring cleaning, scanning line boundaries via `strings.IndexByte` and using a pre-allocated `strings.Builder` reduces the heap allocations to exactly 1 (and 0 for the fast-path), yielding up to 9-75x lower latency.
**Action:** Always prefer basic, pre-allocated string scanning with `strings.IndexByte` and `strings.Builder` over regular expressions or slice-splitting functions like `strings.Split` in performance-critical or high-throughput string-manipulation loops.
