## 2026-08-08 - Fast-path and Builder-based Parsing over Regex for YAML Formatting
**Learning:** Replaced regular expressions and strings.Split/strings.Join inside hot loop formatting utilities (UnquoteYAMLKey and CleanYAMLNullValues) with high-performance scanner functions using strings.Contains fast-paths, strings.IndexByte, and strings.Builder. Avoided heavy multi-line string allocation and regex matching overhead entirely for standard strings. This resulted in a ~98.1% latency reduction in UnquoteYAMLKey (from ~18.4µs to ~274ns) and a ~88.9% latency reduction in CleanYAMLNullValues (from ~4.9µs to ~269ns), dropping heap allocations to 1 per call.
**Action:** Always prefer basic string scanning techniques, custom scanners, and builder pre-allocation over generic regular expression substitutions or multi-pass split/join arrays in critical paths.
